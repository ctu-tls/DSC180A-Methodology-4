## Quarter 2 Project Reflections

**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

I learned how to use physics-based simulation to simulate a drone and train its control systems using reinforcement learning. I also learned that there are many different models that can be applied to achieve the same goals. 

**2. Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

I would like to investigate different reinforcement learning approaches to improve quadrotor stability and trajectory tracking under changing wind conditions. One possible direction is further training each model to fit different kinds of environment and introduce more external variance. 

**3. What is a potential change you'd make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**

One change I would consider is establishing a more standardized experimental framework. Our current work explores different simulation environments and approaches in parallel, which makes comparisons and integration challenging. Defining our environments more clearly and have a clearer goal of what we are measuring is much better for comparing between models in the future. 

**4. What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**

I am interested in exploring more types of reinforcement learning algorithms, especially trying to add large language models onto the controllers andI am interested in seeing how that approach can bring us different results as to before.
